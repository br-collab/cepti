# Engagement Framework for Social Media Content

## Structure: Hook → Benefit → Social Proof → CTA

### 1. HOOK (First 1-2 sentences)
- Grab attention immediately
- Ask a question, make a bold statement, or create curiosity
- Make the reader STOP scrolling
- Examples:
  - "Your [current problem] just became obsolete."
  - "What if you could [desired outcome] in [time frame]?"
  - "Nobody talks about this, but..."

### 2. BENEFIT (Next 2-4 sentences)
- Explain what CHANGES for the customer
- Focus on transformation, not features
- Answer: "Why should I care?"
- Use problem-solution-benefit arc
- Make it relatable and specific

### 3. SOCIAL PROOF (1-2 sentences)
- Build credibility and trust
- Reference results, durability, quality, satisfaction
- Can be implied or explicit
- Builds authority and confidence

### 4. CTA (Final sentence)
- Clear, specific call-to-action
- Easy to understand next step
- Creates urgency or opportunity mindset
- Examples:
  - "DM us for a free consultation"
  - "Click the link to see it in action"
  - "Tag someone who needs this"
  - "Limited availability—inquire today"

## Platform-Specific Tuning
- **Facebook**: Focus on trust, testimonials, community
- **Instagram**: Visual storytelling, aspiration, lifestyle
- **Threads**: Conversational, insider knowledge, opinion

## Length Guidelines
- **Facebook**: 150-300 characters optimal for engagement
- **Instagram**: 150-200 primary text, hashtags after line break
- **Threads**: 100-150 characters for conversational feel
